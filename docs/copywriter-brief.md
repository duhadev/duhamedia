# Duha Media website: copy to write

**For:** our copywriter. **Prepared:** 2026-11-07, from the live site (duhamedia.com) as it stands today.

## How to use this document

Every row below is a slot on the live website that still has stand-in text. Write your copy in the **New copy** column (or in a copy of this file) and send it back. We paste it into the site's editor, so you don't need a login.

- **Each row has an ID** (for example `SVC-1`). Use it when you send copy back or ask a question.
- **Text in [square brackets] is a stand-in.** The site must not show a single bracket when it launches.
- **"STRATEGIC PLACEHOLDER — final copy TBD"** marks the main headline and intro on a page. The text after the label is our strategic draft. It says what the slot has to accomplish. Rewrite it, or keep it if it already works, but drop the label either way.
- **Rows marked FACT** need a real number or fact from Abdurrahman (the founder). Write the sentence around it, but never guess the number. Put `[ASK]` where it goes and list it in Part A.
- **Length:** stay close to the current length unless the Notes say otherwise. The layouts were designed around these lengths, and a headline twice as long will wrap badly on a phone.

### Accent phrase (read this before rewriting any headline)

Many headlines show one phrase in the brand accent colour (on the site: "Don't take our word for it. **Take theirs.**"). That phrase is stored separately as the *accent phrase*, and it **must appear word for word inside the headline**. When you rewrite a headline, also give us the new accent phrase: usually the last 1 to 4 words, the part that lands. Rows that have one show it as *Accent: "…"*.

### House rules (these are binding, not style preferences)

1. **Voice:** direct, evidenced, operator-confident, unsentimental. Short sentences. Numbers are the headline, not decoration.
2. **Don't invent facts:** no made-up client names, results, ratings, founder history or policy terms. A missing fact stays `[ASK]`.
3. **Pricing appears only as a small inline note:** "Builds start at $X" and "Engines start at $1K/mo". Never write a pricing section or a price table. The one exception is the first FAQ answer, which gives the price directly.
4. **Every capacity number gets its reason.** "7 clients max" always comes with a one-line *why* under it. A bare number reads as fake urgency. No countdowns and no "only 2 spots left!".
5. **No "coming soon."** Where there's nothing to show yet (case studies, reviews), the page shows a designed alternative. Write that copy so it reads as complete, not as an apology.
6. **Leave button labels short:** Book a Call, Contact us, Join.
