Ad spend and influencer costs are paid by you, separately. Online revenue is the total money taken through your online store, not counting refunds, chargebacks, shipping and losses. Online profit is online revenue minus the cost of the products sold.

We build and grow your store for one reason: we want your business to succeed. Everything we charge is set up to help you grow, not hold you back.
