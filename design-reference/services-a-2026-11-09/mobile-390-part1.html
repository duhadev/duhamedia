Service 02 · Grow your store

Then we grow it.

Marketing, run by the same team that built your store. Every plan includes the full toolkit.

A new store doesn't sell itself. Someone has to bring people to it, and keep bringing them.

We start with paid ads, then shift effort between tools based on what the numbers show. If the budget is too small to scale ads, more goes into organic content and creators.

You made something great. Now let's make sure people know.

Every month
1 hr

How it's paid
One of three plans, settled quarterly. Ad spend and influencer costs are paid by you, separately. See plans

Paid ads

  • Meta ads. Facebook and Instagram campaigns. This is where we start.
  • Campaign landing pages. One per campaign, so the ad and the store say the same thing.

Organic social and creators

  • Social posting. Facebook, Instagram, Reels and TikTok, filmed with you each month.
  • Collaborations. We scout, reach out and manage partnerships. Nothing is sent without your approval.

Search, email and print

  • Google Business Profile. Search and Maps, with review growth and prompt replies.
  • SEO and GEO. Showing up when people search, and when they ask AI tools, for what you sell.
  • Newsletter. Sent through Shopify Email.
  • Flyers. On-brand, on request.
