Who it's for

Built for a proven product.

Plenty of local businesses have the hard part figured out. The product is proven, the regulars keep coming back, and word of mouth does its work. Then a new customer lands on the online store, and it doesn't measure up.

That gap is what a build closes. We build the Shopify store your product has earned, around your logo, your colors and your customers.

Your product is proven. Your online store should be too.

Right for you if

  • You run an owner-operated business.
  • Your product is proven. The regulars keep coming back.
  • Your online store doesn't show it yet.
  • You want to own the store, the code and the domain.

Not for you if

  • You're still testing whether the product sells.
  • You want a template store for less than $7K.
  • You need the store live in days, not about 5 weeks.

Not sure? On a free 45-minute call we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.

Do you use templates?

No. Every store is designed and coded from scratch.

How long does a build take?

About 5 weeks. A build takes about 55 hands-on hours, about 30 of them on design alone.

I already have a store. Do I have to start over?

No. We can revise the store you have: keep what's working, and redesign and rebuild what isn't. If it needs more than that, we'll build a new theme from scratch. We'll tell you which on the call.

Do you use AI?

Yes, in our build pipeline, to code faster. The design is still planned and approved with you.

Can I hire you for just the build?

Yes. The marketing steps are optional.

How do sign-offs work?

For the build, you sign off twice: payment to start, and the finished theme build. Nothing moves to the next stage without your approval.

Who does the work?

The founder, in-house. No middlemen. We cap at 7 clients so every store gets the hours it needs.
