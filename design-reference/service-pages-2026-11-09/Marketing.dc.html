Who it's for

A new store doesn't sell itself.

Someone has to bring people to it, and keep bringing them. You built the product. Our job is to put it in front of the people who haven't found you yet.

Every plan comes with the full toolkit ready to go: ads, social content, creators, search and email. We use each tool when your store needs it, and we use it well.

How a month runs

About 5 hours of yours. The rest is ours.

One call to plan, one session to film. Then we run it all month and let the numbers decide next month's plan.

  1. 01 · With you

    Plan

    A planning call, up to an hour, usually less. We agree what to film, post and run.

  2. 02 · With you, in person

    Film

    3–4 hours. Scripts and articles get written in the same session, so the month's content is ready when it ends.

  3. 03 · Us, all month

    Post and run ads

    We post the content, run the campaigns and manage collaborations. Nothing goes to a creator without your approval.

  4. 04 · Us, then you

    Read the numbers

    We shift effort between tools based on what the numbers show, and bring it to next month's call.

Online revenue is the total money taken through your online store, not counting refunds, chargebacks, shipping and losses. Online profit is gross profit.

At $100,000 a year

Every plan switches to a fixed rate once your online store reaches $100,000 a year. The rate is set for your store, based on the work it needs, and agreed in writing.

Switching early

When your quarterly results show you're on pace, we offer the switch early. It's your choice. If you wait, it's required once the revenue is reached.

The agreement

Two-quarter terms that renew. Either side can cancel with notice before the next term. You own all content, ads and accounts. No guaranteed results.

We build and grow your store for one reason: we want your business to succeed. Everything we charge is set up to help you grow, not hold you back.

Marketing questions

Straight answers.

All questions →

Is there a minimum term for marketing?

Yes: two quarters. That's long enough to film, run ads and see what works before deciding. After that it renews for two quarters at a time, and you can cancel with notice before the next term.

Can I just get ads?

Every plan includes the full toolkit, and paid ads are where we start. If the numbers show organic content or collaborations will do more for your store, we shift effort there. You pay the same plan either way.

Who pays for ads?

You do. Ad spend, influencer costs and other marketing expenses are separate from our fees.

Do you lock clients into retainers?

No. The marketing runs on two-quarter terms that renew, and either side can cancel with notice before the next term. You own everything we make for you: the store, the code, the domain, the content and the accounts.

What happens at $100,000 a year?

Your plan switches to a fixed rate, set for your store based on the work it needs.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We watch the numbers and shift effort to what works.
