Specification — what each channel is for
E-01 Email The list you already own — built first because it pays for the rest. +
What we run

Welcome, browse-abandon, cart, post-purchase and win-back flows, plus a campaign calendar built around what actually sells. Segmentation from the CRM, not guesswork.

What you'll see on the dashboard

Revenue per send, flow revenue split from campaign revenue, list growth and churn. Email is the first line because it's the cheapest evidence the engine works.

E-02 Paid ads Spend scales only on tracking that tells the truth. +
What we run

Meta and Google, structured around your margins. Creative tested against the content we shoot in E-03. If attribution is broken we fix it before a dollar moves.

What you'll see on the dashboard

Spend, blended return, and cost per new customer next to repeat revenue — so a "good ROAS" can't hide an unprofitable account.

E-03 Social / content We show up with a camera — shot at your business, not stock. +
What we run

Scheduled shoot days on site: product, place, people. One shoot feeds organic posts, ad creative, email and product pages — the same asset working four times.

What you'll see on the dashboard

Which assets earned attention and which earned money. Content is judged on the ad account and the store, not on follower count.

E-04 SEO Compounding capture on the Phase 01 foundations. +
What we run

Category and product page work first, then the articles that answer what buyers actually ask. Built on the structure and speed shipped in Phase 01, so nothing is retrofitted.

What you'll see on the dashboard

Non-brand traffic and the revenue behind it, tracked monthly. Slow by design — the line that makes month twelve cheap.

E-05 GBP For hybrid businesses the profile is a storefront. Run like one. +
What we run

Categories, hours, products and photos kept current, posts on a schedule, and a review routine your staff can actually keep. Same discipline as the ads account.

What you'll see on the dashboard

Calls, direction requests and profile-driven visits alongside online orders — local demand counted, not assumed.

E-06 CRM Repeat purchase decides whether ads are profitable. +
What we run

One customer record wired to the store, email and ads: segments, lifecycle stages, and the lists every other channel targets from.

What you'll see on the dashboard

Repeat rate, time between orders and lifetime value by cohort — the quiet numbers that decide how hard you can afford to spend.

You can't buy ads alone — the mix is priced on the call.
