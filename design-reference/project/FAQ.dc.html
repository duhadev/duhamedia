Pricing & Contracts

Builds start at $5K. Engines start at $1K/mo. Exact scope is priced on the call — because a $500K hybrid retailer and a $5M online brand need different machines, and pretending otherwise is how agencies pad quotes. No — the endgame is your team running it. Handover looks like: documented playbooks, your staff trained on the dashboard, and a scheduled final month. Phase 3 is on the calendar from day one. Because packages hide scope. You get a fixed number on the call, in writing, before any work starts. No surprises after. Builds are fixed-scope projects. Engines run month-to-month after a 6-month minimum — long enough to judge a compounding machine honestly.

Builds

You approve at checkpoints — positioning, design, pre-launch — so there's no surprise reveal. Disagreements get settled with data, not taste debates.

Marketing

Process & Trust

If you net $500K–$5M a year and sell something people already buy, probably. If not, we'll tell you on the call — a bad-fit client costs us a capacity slot. You'll see it in the same dashboard we do, monthly, in writing. We change the plan or we part ways — you're never paying to hide a problem. Because the cap is our quality gate, not a countdown timer. Current load is stated on the homepage and updated as it changes. The last one disappeared after launch. Our engagement is structured so that leaving is the deliverable — Phase 3 is a handover, documented and scheduled.
