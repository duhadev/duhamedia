Q. What do you actually do?

We build the store, operate the growth engine, and then train your team to run it. Three phases, one team, one dashboard. Most agencies do one of those three and hand you a report about it.

Q. Who is this for?

Established businesses netting $500K–$5M — physical, online, or hybrid. They already know how to sell offline. Online they're under-scaled, and usually they've been burned once already.

Q. What happens in month one?

An audit, and nothing shipped. Tracking gets rebuilt before anything is spent, because every later decision is made on those numbers — and because scaling spend into a store that leaks demand just buys more leaks.

Q. When do you leave?

Phase 3, typically around month nine. We train your team, document the machine, hand over the accounts. The goal is your team running this without us — that's the sentence, and it's scheduled, not implied.

Q. Why cap at seven builds?

Capacity is our quality gate. Seven concurrent builds is what keeps every one of them senior-staffed. Three are in progress right now.

Q. What don't you do?

We don't take a build we can't staff, we don't run ads into a store we haven't measured, and we don't publish a number a client hasn't approved. If a claim can't be checked, we don't make it.
