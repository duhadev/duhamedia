01

What breaks first

Your store already has demand — offline revenue proves it. What leaks it is rarely the homepage. In audits it's the checkout step count, blocking app scripts before first paint, and tracking that double-counts returning customers so the reports overstate paid by a third or more.

None of those are design problems. That is why a developer hired on portfolio alone tends to hand back a prettier version of the same leak.

02

The build checklist

Demand this list in writing from anyone you hire. Three items on it are routinely left out, and all three are expensive to retrofit.

Theme decision on the record. Customized $300 theme or custom build, with the revenue threshold that justifies the difference.
Tracking before traffic. GA4, pixel, server-side events, tested with real orders before launch — not after the first campaign.
SEO decisions you can't undo cheaply. URL structure, collection architecture, migration redirects, mapped before build.
Performance budget. A stated first-paint target, with the app audit that keeps it.
Handover. Documentation and training so your team edits the store without a ticket.
03

What it costs

Chicago rates spread wide. Freelancers land $2–6K and disappear at launch; full agencies quote $25K+ and staff you juniors. We start at $5K for a Revision and $12K for a scratch build, fixed after the audit.

The cheapest build is the one you don't pay for twice.

Short answer

Between $5K and $25K for a build worth having. Revisions of an existing store start at $5K; scratch builds start at $12K. Below $2K you are buying a theme install, and above $25K you are usually paying for account management, not engineering.

01

Why the range is that wide

Two builds at the same price can differ by everything that isn't visible. The band you land in is set by whether tracking, SEO structure and a performance budget are inside the scope, or quoted later as extras once you're committed.

02

What actually moves the number

Product catalogue size and variant logic. Migration redirects from an old platform. Whether checkout can stay standard. Custom apps or subscriptions. Everything else — page count, "design rounds" — is smaller than it sounds in a proposal.
