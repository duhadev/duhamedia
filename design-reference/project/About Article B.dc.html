01 — The pattern we kept seeing

The businesses we work with aren't startups. They net $500K–$5M a year and already know how to sell — offline. Their store isn't broken so much as unattended: it takes orders, it just doesn't grow.

Two failure modes produce almost every call we take. Five different freelancers, each holding a slice of the account and none holding the whole number. Or an agency that launched the site and disappeared, leaving a theme nobody on staff can edit.

Your store already has the demand. It's leaking it.

03 — The handover is the point

Phase 3 trains your team, documents the machine, and hands it over. It's stated as fact and scheduled as a phase, because retainers that never end are a business model — just not ours.

We cap at seven concurrent builds. Not scarcity theatre: it's what keeps every build senior-staffed. If a claim we make can't be checked on your own dashboard, we don't make it.
