Metric renders one number as a designed element; MetricStrip bands several (the proof fallback while no case studies are live).

```jsx
<Metric value="3.2×" label="Revenue in 12 months" accent size="lg"/>
<MetricStrip items={[{value:"3",label:"Builds in progress",sub:"Current load, transparently"},{value:"7",label:"Client capacity, max",sub:"Capacity is our quality gate"}]}/>
```

At most one `accent` metric per view. `sub` is where urgency claims keep their quiet reason.
